Shutdown and reboot delay

Allow the board enough time to distinguish a reboot from a completed shutdown before removing host power.

Advanced configuration. The board uses eight task ticks per second, but its software-managed timing is not precise. Too short a delay can interrupt a reboot; a longer delay keeps the host powered for longer after shutdown.
